# WAYSTEA ONE
# AI Brain Architecture

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

This document describes the intelligence architecture of WAYSTEA ONE.

WAYSTEA ONE is designed as an AI digital employee with memory, reasoning capabilities and proactive behavior.

The system should not only react to commands.

It should understand context, remember experience and help improve business operations.

---

# 2. AI Core Concept

WAYSTEA ONE consists of several intelligent layers:

- Event Engine
- Decision Engine
- Memory Engine
- Knowledge Engine
- Learning Engine
- Reflection Engine

Together they create the AI brain of the digital employee.

---

# 3. Event Engine

Event Engine is responsible for detecting everything happening inside the company.

Sources of events:

- Telegram messages;
- employee actions;
- completed tasks;
- missed tasks;
- purchases;
- shift changes;
- owner decisions;
- operational problems.

---

Examples:

Employee:

"Закончился Шу Пуэр"

↓

Event:

PRODUCT_OUT_OF_STOCK

↓

AI analyzes situation.

↓

Creates purchase request.

---

Employee:

"Черёмушки на месте"

↓

Event:

SHIFT_STARTED

↓

AI starts shift workflow.

---

# 4. Decision Engine

Decision Engine determines what action AI should take.

Before acting, AI evaluates:

1. What happened?

2. Is there an existing rule?

3. Is there previous experience?

4. Can AI solve this independently?

5. Does owner involvement require?

---

Decision levels:

---

## Level 1 — Autonomous Action

AI acts independently.

Examples:

- send reminders;
- create tasks;
- collect purchase requests;
- generate reports.

---

## Level 2 — Action + Notification

AI acts and informs owner.

Examples:

- repeated employee delays;
- frequent product shortages;
- recurring problems.

---

## Level 3 — Owner Approval Required

AI asks owner.

Examples:

- changing standards;
- financial decisions;
- employee evaluation decisions;
- strategic changes.

---

# 5. Memory Engine

Memory is a core feature of WAYSTEA ONE.

The AI must remember important information.

Memory should not be one large database.

Memory must be separated by purpose.

---

# 6. Types of Memory

## 6.1 Short-Term Memory

Temporary operational context.

Examples:

- today's shifts;
- current tasks;
- active problems.

Purpose:

Manage current operations.

---

## 6.2 Store Memory

Information about each location.

Examples:

Store:

Cheremushki

Memory:

- employee list;
- typical problems;
- customer behavior;
- operational specifics.

---

## 6.3 Employee Memory

Information about employees.

Examples:

- experience level;
- completed training;
- strengths;
- common mistakes;
- communication history.

Purpose:

Better assistance and training.

---

## 6.4 Owner Memory

Important owner decisions.

Examples:

"The quality of tea is more important than reducing cost."

"We never sell low-quality tea."

Purpose:

AI follows business philosophy.

---

## 6.5 Company Memory

WAYSTEA knowledge base.

Contains:

- standards;
- instructions;
- tea knowledge;
- service principles;
- company values.

---

# 7. Knowledge Engine

Knowledge Engine stores structured business information.

Examples:

Products:

- tea descriptions;
- brewing methods;
- prices.

Operations:

- opening procedures;
- cleaning standards;
- customer service rules.

Training:

- employee education materials.

---

# 8. Learning Engine

WAYSTEA ONE improves through experience.

Learning sources:

- owner corrections;
- approved decisions;
- successful solutions;
- repeated problems.

---

Example:

AI suggests:

"Increase GABA stock."

Owner approves.

System remembers:

"This purchasing recommendation was successful."

---

# 9. Reflection Engine

Every day the AI analyzes company activity.

Questions:

What happened today?

What problems appeared?

What repeated?

What can be improved?

What should owner know?

---

Example:

"Three employees asked the same question about brewing Da Hong Pao. Recommend creating a training instruction."

---

# 10. Proactive Intelligence

WAYSTEA ONE should not wait for problems.

It should detect patterns.

Examples:

"The same task is delayed every Monday."

"Tea consumption increased by 30% this month."

"New employee requires additional training."

---

AI should provide recommendations, not make uncontrolled decisions.

---

# 11. Human-Like Communication

WAYSTEA ONE should communicate naturally.

The AI should understand:

- informal messages;
- short answers;
- mistakes;
- different wording.

Examples:

Employee:

"сделал"

"готово"

"всё"

"ок"

AI understands these as possible completion confirmations.

---

# 12. AI Personality Rules

WAYSTEA ONE should be:

- friendly;
- calm;
- respectful;
- supportive;
- professional.

---

The AI should avoid:

- aggressive language;
- blaming;
- unnecessary messages;
- excessive control.

---

# 13. Error Handling

If AI does not know something:

It must not invent an answer.

Correct behavior:

"I don't have enough information. I will ask for clarification."

---

# 14. AI Safety Rules

WAYSTEA ONE cannot:

- fire employees;
- punish employees;
- change company rules;
- make financial decisions;
- hide problems.
