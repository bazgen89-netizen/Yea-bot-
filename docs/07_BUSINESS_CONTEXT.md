# WAYSTEA ONE
# Business Context

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Company Overview

WAYSTEA is a premium Chinese tea company focused on developing tea culture and creating a high-quality customer experience.

The company combines:

- premium Chinese tea;
- tea education;
- tea ceremonies;
- hospitality;
- modern tea spaces.

The company goal is not only selling tea.

The goal is to introduce people to tea culture and create a unique emotional experience.

---

# 2. Business Model

WAYSTEA operates through:

- physical tea stores;
- online sales;
- tea events;
- tastings;
- tea education.
